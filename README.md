
Purdue Events Aggregator

Live prototype: https://bourbonic117.github.io/Purdue-Events/

A prototype that collects campus event listings from several Purdue sources into one searchable, filterable page, and refreshes itself every week.

The problem

Purdue Indianapolis students may miss campus events because information is scattered across BoilerLink, Instagram, CampusBord, the university calendar, and posted flyers. No single channel reaches everyone, so students have to know where to look and keep checking several places. This affects new students who do not yet know where to look, commuters who are on campus less often, and students who want to get involved but are unsure what is happening. The events exist; the gap is that students often do not hear about them in time, or at all.

What the prototype does
Combines sources. Pulls events from the Purdue University events calendar, BoilerLink, and CampusBord into one list.
Removes duplicates. The same event often appears on more than one site. Duplicates are detected and merged, and each event shows every site it was listed on.
Lets students narrow the list. Filters by date, time of day, field of study, source, and practical details (food, virtual, Indianapolis campus), plus keyword search.
Updates itself. A scheduled job rebuilds the page every Monday, with no manual work.
Try it

On the live page, try:

1. Click Next 7 days under Date, then Evening under Time of day.
2. Add the Food chip under Good to know.
3. Click Indianapolis to focus on the Indianapolis campus.
4. Pick a field of study, or type a keyword such as career in the search box.
5. Click Clear all filters to reset.
